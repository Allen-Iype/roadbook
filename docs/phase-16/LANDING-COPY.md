## Three steps

1. **Export your history from Google Maps.** It takes about two minutes
   on your phone and gives you one file. Step-by-step guides for Android
   and iPhone are inside.
2. **Upload that file.** Roadbook reads it and finds the trips that stood
   out — the ones far from home, with somewhere you stayed.
3. **Keep the ones that mattered.** Say yes to the real adventures and name
   them; the rest stay off the map.

No Timeline history? Start from your photos instead. They already know
where and when they were taken.

## Privacy

Your history stays in your own account. Nobody else can see it unless you
share a link, and you can take that link back. Your export is kept so
Roadbook can re-read it later. Photos are never kept, only where and when
each was taken. Delete everything whenever you like. No ads, no trackers.
