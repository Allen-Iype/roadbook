Direction B — Night expedition

Token sheet. The map room at night. Light is evidence: the brighter a line, the more it was actually measured.

LEG-KIND LIGHTS — RESERVED FIRST

The glow is the thesis and it belongs to observed alone. Routed is lit but cold. Unknown is dim and dashed — visible, never luminous; its dimness is its honesty. Air is a violet dotted arc. Kind is never hue alone: glow, dash, and arc geometry are the redundant channels.

SIGNATURE — LIGHT AS EVIDENCE

The glow on observed geometry is the one bold element; everything else is a quiet instrument. Stat tiles, the light-key legend, and day headings are set in mono with hairline rules. Photos, when present, are lit windows on the dark field.

PROVENANCE BAR

Every headline distance carries a bar of its kind-composition (here: Akureyri by air — 53 km observed, 10 routed, 568 air). Invariant 8 applied to numbers, not just geometry.

LEGEND WORDING — IDENTICAL EVERYWHERE

Observed — recorded fixes · Routed — inferred along roads · Unknown — straight line, nothing inferred · Air — great-circle arc
