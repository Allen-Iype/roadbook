The honesty channel at life-map scale

Invariant 8: any geometry on a map carries its leg kind — at every zoom. At country scale, per-leg texture threatens to collapse into noise; flattening each adventure into one confident line is the lie the product refuses. Two treatments survive Stage A. This page renders both, in both aesthetic directions, on the real demo geometry.

Overview panels show the map in the state a self-hoster sees before configuring a router: the Westfjords gaps are unknown — dashed straight lines — because that is where T1 and T2 genuinely diverge. Detail panels show the routed Akureyri region, where the treatments converge.

DIRECTION A — ATLAS PLATE

Overview state: Höfn observed (red ink) · Westfjords unrouted (dashed unknown) · Akureyri flights (dotted arcs) with short observed drives.

DIRECTION B — NIGHT EXPEDITION

Same geometry, same states. The unknown must stay visible on the dark ground without ever glowing — dimness is its honesty.

The question this page decides: T1 keeps observed and routed as distinct hues at every zoom and accepts that the distinction compresses at country scale. T2 concedes the overview to two distinctions that stay legible — known vs unknown, ground vs air — and returns the full four-way split as you zoom in. T2 is a defensible reading of invariant 8 if and only if the overview never renders unknown as confident; both treatments here keep unknown dashed and air arced at all zooms.
