LEG-KIND INKS — RESERVED FIRST

The four kinds own the loudest channel on every surface. Nothing else on the page may use these hues at line weight. Kind is never hue alone: routed carries a paper casing (printed-road symbology), unknown is dashed, air is a dotted arc with a plane mark.

SIGNATURE — THE PLATE MARGIN

Every map is framed by a double rule with its marginalia set into the margin: plate number, graticule ticks, scale bar, and the leg-kind legend. The legend is printed marginalia, not a floating widget — treatment T3 made structural. Days are numbered stages, after the rally roadbook.

PROVENANCE BAR

Every headline distance carries a hairline bar of its kind-composition (here: Akureyri by air — 53 km observed, 10 routed, 568 air). Invariant 8 applied to numbers, not just geometry.

LEGEND WORDING — IDENTICAL EVERYWHERE

Observed — recorded fixes · Routed — inferred along roads · Unknown — straight line, nothing inferred · Air — great-circle arc

BASEMAP NOTE

Terrain-muted light base: Positron-class or a Roadbook-tuned Maputnik style served via ROADBOOK_MAP_STYLE. The basemap’s job is to recede; nothing on it may compete with the leg-kind inks.
